## Current

### Blood pressure estimation from RGB video

_M.Sc. thesis, Sharif University of Technology · 2026 – present_

Developing a deep-learning model that maps remote photoplethysmography (rPPG) signals, extracted from ordinary RGB video, to systolic and diastolic blood pressure, toward contactless cardiovascular monitoring.

### Deep Koopman VAE models of EEG brain dynamics

_Research intern (remote), University of British Columbia · 2026 – present_

- Adapting the BRICK deep Koopman VAE architecture from fMRI to long, high-frequency EEG recordings.
- Incorporating recorded galvanic vestibular stimulation (GVS) as a control input to the latent dynamics.

## Past

### EEG brain network analysis

_B.Sc. thesis, Sharif University of Technology · 2023 – 2024_

- Applied the surface Laplacian as an alternative to source localization.
- Estimated effective connectivity with the phase slope index (PSI).
- Performed statistical analysis of graph measures computed with the Brain Connectivity Toolbox (BCT).

### Multimedia design and functional brain networks

Studied how educational multimedia design principles affect local and global information processing in EEG-derived functional brain networks. Published in [_Scientific Reports_ (2025)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-08611-0).
