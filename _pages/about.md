I am an M.Sc. student in Electrical Engineering at [Sharif University of Technology](https://en.sharif.edu/), where I also received my B.Sc. My research lies at the intersection of **machine learning**, **biomedical signal processing**, and **computational neuroscience**.

For my M.Sc. thesis, I am developing deep-learning models that estimate blood pressure (SBP/DBP) from remote photoplethysmography (rPPG) signals extracted from ordinary RGB video. I am also a remote research intern at the **University of British Columbia**, where I adapt the BRICK deep Koopman VAE architecture from fMRI to long, high-frequency EEG recordings, incorporating galvanic vestibular stimulation (GVS) as a control input.

My B.Sc. thesis focused on EEG functional and effective connectivity, using the surface Laplacian, the phase slope index, and graph-theoretical analysis of brain networks. Related work on how educational multimedia design shapes information processing in functional brain networks was published in _Scientific Reports_ (2025).

**Research interests:** machine learning for biomedical signals, EEG and brain network analysis, computational and theoretical neuroscience, graph signal processing.
